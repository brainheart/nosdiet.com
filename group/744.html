Resist Easter candy? On Easter? That's sacreligous (sp?).

I had been looking forward to Easter candy for a long time. So on Easter I 

went to the store and bought one bag of each of the kinds I remembered from 

childhood: the big, hard, colored egg ones with the white in the middle, 

chocolate malt eggs, and some regular jelly bean eggs. My goal was to eat 

myself sick. When I got home at my first sitting I got too full before I could feel 

bad. I went outside and worked in the garden. At suppertime I came in, had a 

big supper and then attacked the bags again. I had better luck this time. Pretty 

soon I was not feeling so good. So I stopped. I was happy and uncomfortable.

There was a half a bag each ot the three kinds left. I wanted to throw them 

away; my wife took them to the people at her work.

I'm ok now.

I plan on doing the same thing next year. Oh yeah, one more thing - candy 

corn at Halloween . . . .
